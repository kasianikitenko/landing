AI-ASSISTED TRAVEL PLANNING

2025

I led early UX discovery and product direction for an AI-assisted planning platform, connecting user needs, operational workflows, and technical constraints.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Itinerary creation was slow and manual, with planning, content, and supplier coordination spread across fragmented workflows.

02 / THE EXPLORATION

I mapped the end-to-end process, researched user and business needs, and identified where AI could reduce effort without removing necessary human control.

03 / THE PRODUCT DIRECTION

I designed early journeys around natural-language interaction, adaptive content, and review points, then validated the concepts with technical teams.

04 / WHAT CHANGED

Early validation showed a 50% reduction in itinerary-building time and established clearer integration points for AI across the service.

PERSONALISED INVESTING

2022-2024

I led UX for a white-label investment platform for financial intermediaries, combining behavioural research, personalisation, and product design.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

The experience needed to support investors with very different levels of knowledge, confidence, and decision-making behaviour.

02 / THE RESEARCH

I conducted interviews and developed a persona-based personalisation model using behavioural and emotional traits.

03 / THE DESIGN DECISION

Behavioural data and machine learning were used to tailor content, visuals, guidance, and recommendations. I designed and tested the personalised onboarding and recommendation journeys.

04 / WHAT CHANGED

The redesigned onboarding doubled NPS in early testing and established a scalable UX foundation for AI-driven personalisation.

CLINICAL INTELLIGENCE

2019-2021

I led UX for a clinical analytics platform, translating complex operational data into clearer workflows, dashboards, and a scalable design system.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Research specialists needed to navigate large, complex datasets without losing the depth required for operational decisions.

02 / THE RESEARCH

Interviews, usability testing, and UX audits revealed different user needs, decision patterns, and information priorities.

03 / THE DESIGN DECISION

I designed visual summaries, drill-down views, role-specific filters, and scalable navigation to help users move between overview and detail.

04 / WHAT CHANGED

The redesign improved decision-making speed, reduced onboarding complexity, and created reusable interaction patterns adopted across multiple programmes.

MY APPROACH

My approach combines research, design thinking, experience and service strategy, behavioural science, and decision design to shape products around how people actually think, behave, and make choices. I use research to uncover needs, motivations, and friction; design thinking to frame and explore the right problems; and experience and service strategy to connect individual interactions into coherent end-to-end journeys. Behavioural science and decision design help me go a level deeper, understanding how confidence, cognitive load, emotion, context, and choice architecture influence behaviour. Together, these disciplines help me move from insight to strategy to design decisions that are evidence-based, intentional, and built to create meaningful change.
